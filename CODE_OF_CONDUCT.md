# VV2 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We strive to create an open, friendly, and constructive community. As contributors we commit to keeping this project welcoming and harassment‑free for everyone — regardless of age, ethnicity, experience level, race, gender identity, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic. As a simple guideline: keep contributions focused on the menu and project work; unrelated commentary is discouraged.

## Positive Standards

Help build an inclusive environment by:

- Treating everyone with respect and courtesy
- Welcoming new contributors and answering questions patiently
- Respecting differing viewpoints and constructive debate
- Prioritizing what’s best for the project

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following are examples of behaviors that undermine a healthy community and will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Racial slurs or hate speech of any kind
- Political campaigning or imagery (including memes used for political purposes)
- Trolling, doxxing, or abusive behavior
- Posting leaked or copyrighted code
- Promoting unrelated personal projects or recruiting contributors without consent
- Using avatars or images that show affiliation with extremist groups
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional, collaborative setting

## Maintainers’ Responsibilities

Maintainers will:

- Clarify and enforce these standards fairly
- Take appropriate corrective action when necessary (edit/remove content, close PRs, or ban users if needed)
- Act to protect the community’s safety and the project’s integrity

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies this repository alone.

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you encounter abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior, report it.

## Why We Have This

The broader gaming and hacking communities can attract people who harm collaboration and drive away contributors. This Code of Conduct exists to remove those disruptive influences quickly so contributors can focus on building great software.
